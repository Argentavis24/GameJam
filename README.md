# WayOut

A 2D side‑scroller platformer built in Godot 4 for a game jam at SRM Ramapuram.

Theme: Glimmer of Hope

---

## About the Game

WayOut is a 2D side‑scroller platformer where you fight the evil forces of the world using the magic relic “Lantern of Hope”. The Lantern symbolizes your hope to overcome despair against unfathomable odds for the sake of humanity.

Visually, the Lantern reflects your health throughout each level: as you take damage, its light dims, giving you an immediate sense of how close you are to falling.

---

## Story and Themes

You carry the Lantern of Hope through three symbolic stages, each representing a deeper layer of inner and outer struggle:

1. Stage 1 – Negative Thoughts  
   Face death reapers who scythe through hope, representing the crushing weight of negative thoughts.

2. Stage 2 – The 7 Deadly Sins  
   Confront and destroy the 7 deadly sins (as described in the Bible), embodying the moral and spiritual corruption you must overcome.

3. Stage 3 – The Self vs. Self  
   Battle Hades, the King of the Underworld, who guards the gates of hell. He represents beating yourself down in a negative way and trying to prevent the hope you carry from escaping.

---

## Gameplay

- Genre: 2D side‑scroller platformer  
- Engine: Godot 4  
- Core Mechanic:  
  - Run, jump, and fight through enemies across three themed stages.  
  - Manage your health, visually represented by the Lantern of Hope’s light intensity.  
  - Use the Lantern’s power to destroy evil forces and progress.
